# Code of conduct

This project's public spaces — issues, discussions, releases and the website — are for people who build and secure
software. We expect everyone who takes part to:

- be respectful and constructive; disagree with ideas, not with people;
- assume good faith, especially from people reporting problems or asking for help;
- keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and unsolicited advertising;
- not publish other people's private information, including security vulnerabilities before they are fixed
  (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules. Report
behaviour that concerns you to conduct@matelot.io; reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, for a project that does not take code contributions.
